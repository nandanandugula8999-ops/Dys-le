Clinical Handwriting Biomarkers & Dyslexia

Reversals (Mirror Writing)

Persistent kinetic reversals (e.g. b/d, p/q, s/z, 2/5) beyond age 7–8 strongly correlate with visual-spatial dyslexia. The CNN extracts directional orientation markers to detect letter mirroring.

Overwritten / Corrected Strokes

Frequent retracing, hesitation, and mid-character stroke repairs reflect uncertainty in character spatial configuration, common in dysgraphia and compensatory dyslexia strategies.

Multi-Sample Averaging Rationale

Evaluating multiple characters written by the same individual and averaging individual probabilities minimizes false positives caused by natural penmanship quirks, delivering reliable clinical screening.
